## Backend — Stage 1 (Chart of Accounts + Fiscal Years/Accounting Periods)

### Chart of Accounts

Migration `0048_create_chart_of_accounts`. Tree structure modeled directly on Daftra's own chart-of-accounts shape: five immutable root accounts (Assets/Liabilities/Equity/Revenue/Expenses), branching into group accounts (folders) and leaf accounts (the only kind a journal entry line can post to). `is_group` marks a folder; `is_system` is scoped to *only* the five roots — every other seeded account is fully editable/deletable. `normal_balance` is stored explicitly per account (not derived from `account_type`) so contra accounts can carry the opposite normal balance from their category's default.

Default template seeded in the same migration: 48 accounts total — 5 roots, 15 level-2 groups/leaves, 28 level-3 leaves — covering accounts receivable/payable, VAT input/output, inventory, COGS, sales revenue and its two contra accounts, a full operating-expense breakdown, and more.

`ChartOfAccountsService` enforces: a child can only be added under an `is_group = true` parent; the five system roots can never be deactivated or deleted; a group account can't be deleted while it still has children; code uniqueness surfaces as a friendly conflict error. Also exposes `assertPostable(db, accountId)`, which Journal Entries now calls on every line.

### Fiscal Years & Accounting Periods

Migration `0049_create_fiscal_years`. Not in the master document's own §16.6 entity list — added as necessary supporting plumbing for Journal Entries (a double-entry ledger needs a period-close mechanism), called out explicitly as a schema-adjacent addition. Two tables, each with just two states (`open`/`closed`).

`FiscalYearsService.create()` validates `endDate > startDate`, rejects overlapping ranges, and auto-generates one `accounting_periods` row per calendar month. `AccountingPeriodsService` exposes `close()`/`reopen()`, plus `assertOpenForDate(db, date)` — now called by `JournalEntriesService.post()`.

## Backend — Stage 2 (Journal Entries)

Migration `0050_create_journal_entries`: `journal_entries` (header) + `journal_entry_lines` (lines). Key design decisions:

- **One currency per entry, not per line.** Unlike Purchases/Sales documents (where each line's `Money` carries its own currency, user-chosen per document), a journal entry has exactly one currency for the whole entry — always the tenant's own `tenant_settings.currencyCode`. This is a deliberate deviation: a general ledger only makes sense summed in one currency, and no FX-conversion layer exists in the platform yet. `JournalEntriesService` is what reads the tenant's currency and constructs `Money` from the raw minor-units strings the controller receives; the controller never has to guess a currency the way Purchases/Sales controllers do via `moneyFromDto`.
- **Exactly one of debit/credit per line**, enforced twice: a DB `CHECK` constraint on each row, and `JournalEntriesService.buildAndValidateLines()` at the service layer.
- **Total debits = total credits per entry**, enforced only at the service layer (spans multiple rows, so no single-column `CHECK` can express it) — checked on `create()`, `update()`, and again on `post()` as defense in depth.
- **Draft → posted → cancelled lifecycle**, same two-step create/post pattern as every other financial document in this codebase (Purchase/Sales Invoices, Payments Received). A draft is fully editable and deletable and has zero effect on any balance; `post()` is the one-way door.
- **`post()` is the only action gated on the accounting period being open** (`AccountingPeriodsService.assertOpenForDate()`). A draft can be created or edited for any date — CLAUDE.md's period-close semantics are about blocking new *postings*, not new *drafting*.
- **Reversal, not void, for posted entries.** `reverse()` creates a brand-new draft entry with every line's debit/credit swapped and `reversalOfEntryId` set, rather than deleting or voiding the posted original — preserves the audit trail, matches Daftra's own draft/final-only precedent (no destructive void documented).
- **Every account referenced by a line must be postable** — a leaf (`isGroup = false`), active account, enforced via `ChartOfAccountsService.assertPostable()`.
- Entry numbers are allocated through `NumberingSequencesService.allocateNext(db, 'journal_entry', null)` — same numbered-document mechanism as every other financial document. No migration seeds a default `numbering_sequences` row for `'journal_entry'`; like every other document type, a tenant admin has to configure one first (the service throws a clear error otherwise) — this is a pure Settings configuration step, not a gap.

`SettingsModule` is now imported by `AccountingModule` (was not needed in Stage 1) — `SettingsModule`'s exports were widened from `[NumberingSequencesService]` to `[NumberingSequencesService, TenantSettingsService]` to support this.

## Backend — Stage 2b (Reports)

`AccountingReportsService` + `AccountingReportsController` (`/accounting-reports/*`), all read-only, all computed on the fly from `journal_entry_lines` + `chart_of_accounts` filtered to `status = 'posted'` only (a draft or cancelled entry never affects a balance):

- **`GET /accounting-reports/general-ledger?accountId=&fromDate=&toDate=`** — دفتر الأستاذ. Every posted line touching one account, in date order, with a running balance and an opening balance computed from everything posted strictly before `fromDate`.
- **`GET /accounting-reports/trial-balance?asOfDate=`** — ميزان المراجعة. Every leaf account with posted activity up to `asOfDate`, its total debit/credit and net balance, plus `isBalanced` (should always be `true` if every posted entry balanced, since it's the same invariant summed).
- **`GET /accounting-reports/income-statement?fromDate=&toDate=`** — قائمة الدخل. Revenue and expense accounts over a date range, net of contra accounts, rolling up to `netIncome`.
- **`GET /accounting-reports/balance-sheet?asOfDate=`** — الميزانية العمومية. Asset/liability/equity balances as of a date, plus a `currentYearEarnings` memo line (net income from the containing fiscal year's start date up to `asOfDate` — computed the same way the income statement computes it, **not** a posted closing entry, since no year-end-closing process exists yet) so `isBalanced` (`assets = liabilities + equity`) can be true before real closing entries exist.

**Design decision worth flagging explicitly**: every report signs its amounts using a new `categoryCanonicalSide(accountType)` helper (asset/expense are debit-canonical, liability/equity/revenue are credit-canonical) — **instead of** each account's own `normalBalance` column. This was caught during design, not after: using each account's own `normalBalance` for report arithmetic would make a contra account (e.g. code 42, "Sales Returns & Allowances," `accountType = 'revenue'` but `normalBalance = 'debit'`) sum with the *wrong* sign into the revenue total, since it's still categorized as revenue for reporting purposes even though it behaves like a debit account. Using the category's canonical side uniformly fixes this: the contra account's mostly-debited balance correctly computes as negative revenue. `normalBalance` itself remains stored chart-of-accounts metadata (used by `ChartOfAccountsService`), not report arithmetic.

## Backend — Stages 6/7 (Auto-posting: COGS and Sales-Return revenue reversal)

Built together per the user's explicit direction after two prior architectural blockers were resolved in the same pass (see "Two technical problems solved first" below). This is the first time journal entries are created automatically rather than only by hand.

### Two technical problems solved first

Stages 6/7 were previously blocked (see `claude/accounting-module-research.md`'s roadmap) on two open technical problems. Both were solved before any auto-posting code was written:

1. **Stock-movement events upgraded from plain `EventEmitter2` to Outbox.** `sales.delivery.confirmed` (in `DeliveriesService.confirm()`) and the new `sales.sales_credit_note.issued` (in `SalesReturnsService.confirm()`, see below) now write an outbox row in the same DB transaction as the status change, exactly like `PurchaseInvoicesService.post()`'s existing precedent — the controller no longer calls `events.publish()` directly for these actions; `OutboxDispatcherService` is what puts the event on the bus, on its own schedule, with retry.
2. **Sales Returns given a real financial document.** Per the user's explicit choice ("إشعار دائن مالي حقيقي"), confirming a Sales Return now also creates a **Sales Credit Note** — see the new entity below, fully documented in `claude/sales-module-status.md`.

### The race-condition problem, and why Inventory publishes its own cost events

A key design question: how does Accounting learn the COGS unit cost for a delivery, reliably? The naive approach — Accounting listening to `sales.delivery.confirmed` directly and reading Inventory's `stock_movements` table — is a genuine race condition: `OutboxDispatcherService.emitAsync()` fires every `@OnEvent` listener registered for an event name with **no ordering guarantee** between them. Inventory's own stock listener for that same event might not have finished writing its movement rows yet when Accounting's listener runs.

**Resolution:** Inventory's stock listeners (`DeliveryStockListener`, `SalesReturnStockListener`), immediately after successfully recording their stock movements, now publish their **own** new Outbox-backed events carrying the cost data directly in the payload:

- `inventory.stock_consumption.recorded` — published by `DeliveryStockListener`, one event per delivery confirmation, with per-line quantity and `unitCost` (the location's weighted-average cost at the moment of consumption — `StockMovementsService.applyOutgoing()` always sets a stock-out movement's `unitCost` to `current.averageCost`, so this is exactly the COGS unit cost, reused as-is rather than recomputed).
- `inventory.stock_restoration.recorded` — published by `SalesReturnStockListener`, mirror event for stock coming back in from a sales return, `unitCost` taken from the location's `averageCost` at restoration time.

Accounting's listener reacts to *Inventory's derived event*, never to the original `sales.delivery.confirmed`/return event directly — so it never races with Inventory's own listener for the same source event.

**Atomicity inside the Inventory listeners:** `StockMovementsService.recordMovement()` always opens its own internal `db.transaction()`. To keep "record the movement" and "write the cost outbox event" truly atomic despite that, both listeners now wrap their calls in one **outer** `db.transaction()` — Kysely 0.29.5 (confirmed from `apps/api/package.json`) supports nested transactions as savepoints, so `recordMovement()`'s internal transaction nests correctly inside the listener's own outer one. The existing outer try/catch (log-and-swallow, "needs manual reconciliation") is unchanged — these are still plain-`EventEmitter2` listeners on the *original* event, so that established error-handling shape stays as-is.

### New: Accounting Settings (default-account mapping)

Migration `0052_create_accounting_settings`: a true DB-enforced singleton (`singleton BOOLEAN` + `UNIQUE(singleton)` + `CHECK(singleton = TRUE)`, same shape as `tenant_settings`), with four nullable FK columns into `chart_of_accounts` (`ON DELETE SET NULL`): `accounts_receivable_account_id`, `inventory_account_id`, `cogs_account_id`, `sales_returns_contra_account_id`. This is the mapping the new auto-posting listeners read to know which leaf account to post to. Extended in Stage 3 — see its own section below.

`KyselyAccountingSettingsRepository.getOrCreate()` auto-populates these on first read by looking up the default template's known codes (`'113'` AR, `'114'` Inventory, `'51'` COGS, `'42'` Sales Returns & Allowances) against the tenant's already-seeded `chart_of_accounts` — not hardcoded in the migration itself, since it needs a live lookup. `AccountingSettingsService`/`AccountingSettingsController` expose `GET`/`PATCH /accounting-settings` (gated `accounting.manage`) so a tenant admin can repoint any of the four if their chart of accounts diverges from the default template.

### New: `AccountingAutoPostingListeners`

`apps/api/src/modules/accounting/infrastructure/events/accounting-auto-posting.listeners.ts` — three `@OnEvent` handlers (this pass added two more, see Stage 3 below), each building one balanced journal entry and posting it via `JournalEntriesService.createAuto()`:

- **`handleStockConsumption`** (`inventory.stock_consumption.recorded`) — Dr COGS / Cr Inventory, for the delivery's total cost.
- **`handleStockRestoration`** (`inventory.stock_restoration.recorded`) — the reverse: Dr Inventory / Cr COGS, for the returned stock's cost.
- **`handleSalesCreditNoteIssued`** (`sales.sales_credit_note.issued`) — Dr Sales Returns & Allowances (contra-revenue) / Cr Accounts Receivable, for the credit note's total amount — the revenue-reversal side of a confirmed Sales Return.

**Errors deliberately propagate — no try/catch.** This is a documented, deliberate asymmetry from Inventory's listeners: these handlers are reached via Outbox-dispatched events specifically so `OutboxDispatcherService`'s retry-on-throw mechanism (up to `MAX_ATTEMPTS = 10`, then `'failed'`) applies. Swallowing an error here would silently defeat that reliability guarantee — if account mapping is missing, or a journal-entry invariant fails, the event must be retried, not lost.

### Idempotency for auto-posted entries

An Outbox-dispatched event can legitimately fire more than once (retries). New `journal_entries.source_reference_type`/`source_reference_id` nullable columns (migration `0051`) plus a **partial unique index** (`WHERE source_reference_type IS NOT NULL AND source_reference_id IS NOT NULL`) give every auto-posted entry a DB-enforced idempotency key, keyed off the triggering event's own entity (e.g. the delivery id, the credit note id, and — Stage 3 — the invoice id).

`JournalEntriesService.createAuto(db, input)` — the only way any of these listeners create a journal entry:

1. Looks up `findBySourceReference(type, id)` first. If a `posted` entry already exists, returns it as a no-op. If a `draft` exists (a prior attempt got as far as `create()` but not `post()`), retries `post()` on it. If it exists in any other status, throws (shouldn't happen for auto-posted entries, which are never manually edited).
2. Otherwise creates the entry (`source: 'auto'`) and posts it inside one transaction, allocating an entry number the normal way.
3. If the `create()` transaction hits a Postgres unique-violation (a concurrent retry raced it), re-reads by source reference and recovers the same way step 1 would — same idiom already used by `tenant_settings.getOrCreate()`.

**Known limitation, tracked, not built yet:** CLAUDE.md §9.2 describes an optional "for review" staging state for auto-generated entries (create as draft, let a human review before posting, if the tenant has that setting enabled). `createAuto()` currently always creates-then-immediately-posts — there is no staging toggle yet, for any of the five handlers now built (Stage 3's two new handlers were built the same way, deliberately, for consistency — see Stage 3's own note below). Flagged explicitly in code comments; not silently skipped.

### New: Sales Credit Note (Sales module entity, drives the revenue-reversal side)

Full detail in `claude/sales-module-status.md`. Summary relevant to Accounting: `SalesReturnsService.confirm()` now creates a `SalesCreditNote` (migration `0053`) inside the same transaction as the status flip, with its own `credit_note_number`, one line per return line, priced from the **original `sales_order_line.unitPrice`** (traced `sales_return_line → delivery_line → sales_order_line`) — deliberately not from whatever Sales Invoice(s) happen to cover the sale, since invoicing status isn't always well-defined at return time. It has no user-facing create/update/delete and no status/lifecycle — it's a one-way-door financial fact, read-only via `GET /sales-credit-notes` and `GET /sales-credit-notes/:id`. `confirm()` writes two outbox events in the same transaction: `sales.sales_return.confirmed` (existing, unchanged — plain event, Inventory's stock-restoration listener still reacts to it) and the new `sales.sales_credit_note.issued` (Outbox-backed — this is what Accounting's `handleSalesCreditNoteIssued` reacts to).

### `accounting_settings` extended (migration `0054`)

Three new nullable FK columns, same `ON DELETE SET NULL` shape as the original four:

- **`revenue_account_id`** — credit side of a posted Sales Invoice. Auto-populated on first `getOrCreate()` from the default template's code `'41'` (إيرادات المبيعات), same mechanism as the four Stage 6/7 fields.
- **`accounts_payable_account_id`** — credit side of a posted Purchase Invoice. Auto-populated from code `'211'` (موردون - حسابات دائنة).
- **`purchase_expense_account_id`** — debit side of a posted Purchase Invoice. **Deliberately NOT auto-populated.** The default chart-of-accounts template (migration `0048`) has no single generic "purchases expense" leaf account — only named operating-expense categories under group `'52'` (salaries, rent, utilities, marketing, office supplies, depreciation, professional fees, bank charges, misc). ## Backend — Stage 4/5 (Cost Centers, Bank Accounts) — this pass

Both stages were explicitly flagged in the research doc's roadmap as "core-scope, architecture-neutral, safe to build without further sign-off," so both were built together in one pass per the user's direction ("يلا بينا نكمل stage 4 ,and 5").

### Cost Centers (Stage 4)

Migration `0055_create_cost_centers`: a flat `cost_centers` table (id, `code` UNIQUE, `name`, `is_active`, `notes`, `custom_fields`, timestamps) — no tree structure, unlike Chart of Accounts, matching the research doc's own description as "a simple tagging dimension." Migration `0056_add_cost_center_to_journal_entry_lines` adds a nullable `cost_center_id UUID REFERENCES cost_centers(id) ON DELETE SET NULL` to `journal_entry_lines` — every journal line can optionally carry a cost-center tag, matching Daftra's own precedent (lines "can carry a cost-center allocation," noted in this module's own competitor research).

`JournalEntryLine`/`CreateJournalEntryLineInput`/`JournalEntryLineToPersist` (domain), `journalEntryLineSchema`/`createJournalEntryLineSchema` (contract), and every construction site of a journal-entry line across `JournalEntriesService` (`buildAndValidateLines()`, and `reverse()`'s `swappedLines`, which a repo-wide grep for every `JournalEntryLineToPersist` construction site caught before considering the field addition complete) were all extended with `costCenterId`. `KyselyJournalEntryLineRepository` extended to select/persist/return it end-to-end (`toDomain()`, `listByJournalEntryId()`, `createMany()`, `listPostedByAccount()`).

**Frontend:** `CostCentersPage`/`CostCentersTab` (DataTable + create/edit/delete dialogs, same shape as every other flat-entity screen in this module) at `/accounting/cost-centers`. `JournalEntryLineItemsEditor` gained a cost-center `<Select>` column per line (optional — a `__none__` sentinel maps to `null`, same nullable-FK pattern used elsewhere in this codebase), sourced from a new `useActiveCostCenters()` hook (mirrors `use-postable-accounts.ts`, filters to `isActive`). `JournalEntryDetailsView` gained a matching read-only column showing `code — name` or `—`.

### Bank Accounts (Stage 5)

Migration `0057_add_reconciliation_to_journal_entry_lines` adds `is_reconciled BOOLEAN NOT NULL DEFAULT FALSE` and `reconciled_at TIMESTAMPTZ` to `journal_entry_lines` — reconciliation state lives on the ledger line itself, not on a separate join table, since a line can only ever belong to one bank account's register (a line's account is fixed once posted). Migration `0058_create_bank_accounts` creates `bank_accounts`: `name`, `bank_name`, `account_number`, `iban` (nullable), `currency`, `chart_of_account_id` (`NOT NULL REFERENCES chart_of_accounts(id)`, `UNIQUE` — enforces one bank account per GL account, since two bank accounts sharing a GL account would make reconciliation ambiguous), `opening_balance_amount` (`BIGINT`, minor units), `opening_balance_date` (nullable), `is_active`, `notes`, `custom_fields`, timestamps.

Per the research doc's own scoping note ("basic reconciliation status, not full statement-import reconciliation"), this stage builds a **register view with a manual reconcile/unreconcile toggle per line** — not bank-statement import or auto-matching, which would be a separate competitive enhancement if wanted later.

`BankAccountsService.getRegister(db, id, fromDate?, toDate?)` builds the register by reusing `JournalEntryLineRepository.listPostedByAccount()` against the bank's linked GL account (rather than introducing a second stored ledger) — seeding the running balance from the bank account's own stored `openingBalance` (a real business fact, not zero) and signing each line via the same `categoryCanonicalSide(accountType)` helper `AccountingReportsService.generalLedger()` already uses, after looking up the linked account's real `accountType` through `ChartOfAccountsService.getById()`. This was caught and fixed during this pass — the first draft assumed the linked account is always debit-canonical (asset-side), which would have signed a bank account linked to a liability-type account (e.g. an overdraft facility) backwards.

`BankAccountsService.setLineReconciled(db, bankAccountId, lineId, reconciled)` first verifies the target line actually belongs to the bank account's own linked GL account — defense against reconciling an arbitrary journal line by guessing its id under the wrong bank account's URL. `KyselyJournalEntryLineRepository.setReconciled()` looks up the parent journal entry's real `currency` via `executeTakeFirstOrThrow()` (the FK guarantees it always exists) rather than falling back to a hardcoded default — this repeats the same "never guess a currency" discipline already fixed once before in this module (see the Stage 2/3 `EditJournalEntryForm` fix).

`BankAccountsService` has no delete-guard either, for the same reason as Cost Centers: deleting a `bank_accounts` row only removes descriptive/reconciliation metadata — the real ledger data lives in `chart_of_accounts`/`journal_entry_lines`, untouched by the delete.

`BankAccountsController` exposes CRUD at `/bank-accounts`, plus `GET /bank-accounts/:id/register?fromDate=&toDate=`, `POST /bank-accounts/:id/lines/:lineId/reconcile`, and `POST /bank-accounts/:id/lines/:lineId/unreconcile` — all gated by `accounting.manage`.

**Frontend:** `BankAccountsPage`/`BankAccountsTab` (DataTable + create/edit/delete + a "view register" dropdown action opening `BankAccountRegisterView` in a dialog) at `/accounting/bank-accounts`. `BankAccountRegisterView` follows the same explicit-"Run"-button date-filter pattern as the four `AccountingReportsService` reports (via `useBankAccountRegister(bankAccountId, runParams)`, which only fires once the caller has pressed Run), rendering entry date/number/description/debit/credit/running-balance columns plus a reconciled/unreconciled badge and a reconcile/unreconcile toggle button per line (`useReconcileLine()`/`useUnreconcileLine()`, both invalidate the register query wholesale on success rather than patching one line's balance client-side, to avoid duplicating the service's own signing logic in the frontend).

`CreateBankAccountForm` deliberately excludes `openingBalance`/`openingBalanceDate` from its Zod-validated RHF schema, converting them by hand in `onSubmit` via `decimalToMinorUnits` instead. This was a deliberate design choice made after spotting what looks like a **pre-existing, unrelated bug** elsewhere in the codebase — flagged, not fixed (see "Notable finding, not fixed" below).

### Notable finding, not fixed (out of scope this pass)

While building `CreateBankAccountForm`, `journal-entry-reverse-form.tsx`'s `reversalDate` field was noticed to bind directly to `reverseJournalEntrySchema`'s `reversalDate: isoDate.optional()` with `defaultValues: { reversalDate: '' }`.
